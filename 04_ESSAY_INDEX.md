   (bottom of this file) before it is drafted.

======================================================================
THE QUALITY TEST
======================================================================

Before creating any essay, answer:

1. Which Book does this belong to?

2. Which Season does it advance?

3. What question from the previous essay does it inherit?

4. What new question does it create?

5. Is this about the central healthcare problem,
   or drifting into a subject for its own sake?

6. Which of my interests is actually necessary here?

7. What evidence is required?
   (Name at least one source where claims need it.)

8. What real-world situation makes the idea concrete?

9. Does it move closer to
   understanding → decision → action → outcome?

10. If the buzzword is removed,
    is the underlying problem still interesting?

Test 10 is especially important for AI, HEOR, RWE,
and digital health.

If "AI" is removed and no interesting healthcare problem
remains, it is an AI post, not a Kartik post.
