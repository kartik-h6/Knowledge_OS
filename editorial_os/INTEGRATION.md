# Integration note

This package is tooling. It does not replace the repository's governing documents, and it should not be read as a second constitution.

## Order of authority

1. `02_EDITORIAL_CONSTITUTION.md` — the writing standard, the banned words, the post structure.
2. `04_ESSAY_INDEX.md` — what the series publishes next, the season it belongs to, and the quality test.
3. `01_KNOWLEDGE_MAP.md` — the canonical domain list.
4. This package — workflows that apply the three above.

Where this package and those documents disagree, those documents win.

## Conflicts found, and how they were resolved

| Issue | Package said | Repository says | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Emoji | "Maximum 1–2 emojis, only when useful" | "No bold, italics, underline, or emoji in the published post" | Emoji rule removed from the package files; the repository rule applies. |
| Hashtags | 3–5 relevant hashtags | Not prohibited; published posts carry them | Kept. |
| Cadence | "established Tuesday/Friday cadence" | Not recorded anywhere in the repository | Reframed as a default to confirm, not an established fact. |
| Series | No awareness of Books, Seasons or permanent numbering | Essay numbers are permanent; the index decides what comes next | Added above the workflows as governing authority. |

## Kept from the package

- The hierarchy: truth, credibility, clarity, original insight, usefulness, memorability, engagement.
- Refusal of AI-detector scoring and of algorithm claims stated as facts.
- Draft-only boundary: nothing is published or sent automatically.
- Healthcare boundaries: no patient-identifiable data, no individualized medical or treatment advice, no implication that AI replaces clinical judgment.

## Deliberately not adopted

- Treating the weekly plan as a volume target. A slot is not a reason to publish; the essay index decides what is written, and an idea without evidence or a distinct contribution is held or dropped.
