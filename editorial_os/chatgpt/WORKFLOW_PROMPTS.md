# Reusable ChatGPT Workflow Prompts

Use these as short commands in the existing project. The project constitution remains in force.

## 1. Develop a LinkedIn post

“Use the LinkedIn post workflow. First identify the central insight, the assumption worth testing, and any factual gaps. Challenge my reasoning before drafting. Preserve my established voice and the Editorial Constitution. Do not invent evidence or experiences. Deliver the strongest complete draft, normally 150–250 words.”

## 2. Write a substantive comment

“Use the comment workflow. Read the post carefully and identify what it actually argues. Write a concise comment that contributes a distinct insight, mechanism, relevant experience, or useful question. Avoid generic praise, forced disagreement, and self-promotion. Do not attribute claims to me that I have not made.”

## 3. Reply to a comment

“Use the reply workflow. Classify the comment only to understand its intent, then respond naturally. Address the substance, acknowledge valid points, clarify any disagreement without defensiveness, and preserve a professional relationship. Do not turn every exchange into a lead.”

## 4. Audit my profile

“Use the profile-audit workflow. Assess whether my profile clearly communicates my Pharmacy Practice foundation, M.Pharm development, healthcare/AI/digital-health interests, research orientation, and TechStudyHub Core building journey. Optimize for credibility and coherent positioning—not a generic sales funnel. Separate evidence-backed recommendations from assumptions.”

## 5. Audit content performance

“Use the content-audit workflow. Analyze the data provided across multiple posts where possible. Consider audience relevance, meaningful discussion, saves/shares, profile visits, and qualitative learning alongside reach and reactions. Do not infer causality from small samples or invent missing metrics. Recommend only changes supported by the evidence.”

## 6. Plan the week

“Use the weekly-plan workflow. Start with the default Tuesday/Friday cadence. Select only ideas with a distinct insight, useful evidence, and a clear audience. Identify which idea should be held or dropped rather than filling slots with weak content.”

## 7. Human editorial review

“Run the human-review workflow on this draft. Check factual integrity, specificity, natural rhythm, clarity, voice, repetition, unsupported certainty, and whether the post says something genuinely useful. Do not score AI-likeness or rewrite simply to evade AI detectors. Return a concise diagnosis and a clean revised version.”

## 8. Repurpose an existing idea

“Use the repurpose workflow. Identify the original thesis and what made it valuable. Adapt it to the requested format or audience without recycling the same wording, exaggerating the claim, or losing nuance. State what is genuinely new in the adaptation.”
