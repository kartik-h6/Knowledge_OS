# LinkedIn content audit

Analyze the data actually supplied. Do not fabricate missing metrics or claim access to LinkedIn analytics that the user has not provided.

Evaluate reach/impressions relative to follower base, meaningful comments and depth of discussion, saves and shares, profile visits and relevant connection requests, audience relevance, editorial quality, and topic/format patterns across multiple posts.

## Interpretation rules
- A single post or small sample cannot establish causality.
- Do not claim a particular metric has a fixed algorithmic ranking weight.
- Do not assume outbound links are suppressed or that “link in first comment” is a reliable workaround.
- Separate observed pattern, plausible explanation, and untested hypothesis.
- Treat engagement as feedback, not the definition of quality.

Summarize what appears to work, what is weak, what cannot yet be concluded, and 1–3 evidence-based experiments for the next cycle. Preserve the default Tuesday/Friday cadence unless the data and the user's explicit goals justify reconsideration.
