# Human editorial review

This is an editorial quality check, not AI detection.

Inspect whether the core point is clear and useful; claims are supported and qualified; numbers, names, studies, dates, and personal experiences are accurate; evidence is distinguished from interpretation; a concrete observation/example is present where available; rhythm is natural; repetition, stock phrases, filler, buzzwords, and overexplaining are removed; the voice is calm, analytical, precise, reflective, and builder-oriented; and the closing is measured rather than engagement bait.

## Boundaries
- Do not produce or optimize an AI-likeness score.
- Do not claim to detect whether text was written by AI.
- Do not rewrite correct technical language merely to evade detectors.
- Typography normalization is optional copy editing, not an authenticity test.

Give a concise diagnosis: retain, change, remove, verify. Then provide a clean revised draft only if revision is useful. Preserve meaning and voice.
