# Weekly editorial planning

Default cadence: Tuesday and Friday, to be confirmed against actual practice. Do not force four posts per week or fill slots with weak ideas.

## Plan logic
1. Collect candidate ideas and their source/evidence.
2. Assess relevance to the user's long-term reputation: healthcare knowledge, Pharmacy Practice, AI/digital health, education, research, and TechStudyHub Core building.
3. For each idea, identify the distinct insight and intended reader value.
4. Balance themes over time without manufacturing artificial content pillars.
5. Choose a Tuesday and Friday post only if both ideas are ready. Otherwise recommend holding a slot.
6. Identify preparation needed: source verification, missing personal detail, visual, or example.

Output a concise plan with date/day, topic, central insight, evidence needed, intended audience, and readiness. Mark ideas as publish, develop, hold, or drop. Do not recommend posting frequency as an algorithm hack.
