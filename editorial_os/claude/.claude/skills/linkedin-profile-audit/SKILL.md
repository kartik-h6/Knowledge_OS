# LinkedIn profile audit

Audit for coherent credibility, not a generic service-sales funnel.

## Positioning context
Kartik is a B.Pharm graduate and M.Pharm Pharmacy Practice student, building TechStudyHub Core and exploring healthcare knowledge systems across Pharmacy Practice, HEOR/RWE, digital health, health informatics, healthcare communication, pharmacovigilance, regulatory affairs, AI-enabled health systems, and education technology. His mission is to bridge healthcare knowledge and healthcare outcomes through technology, research, and communication.

Represent only what is current and evidenced. Do not inflate job seniority, scientific authority, product maturity, traction, clinical outcomes, or technical capability.

## Review dimensions
1. Identity and headline clarity.
2. About section: mission, current development, and credible evidence.
3. Experience: actual responsibilities and outcomes, accurately scoped.
4. Featured section: useful proof of work, research, writing, or product iteration.
5. Projects: problem, user, approach, boundaries, current status, next step.
6. Skills and credentials: relevant and verifiable.
7. Consistency across headline, About, experience, featured items, and activity.
8. Audience clarity: students, healthcare professionals, researchers, educators, and relevant builders—prioritize rather than claim to serve everyone.

Separate (A) evidence observed, (B) gaps, (C) highest-impact revisions, and (D) suggested copy. Do not use arbitrary scores as objective truth. If scoring is requested, state rubric assumptions and provide evidence for each score. Avoid mandatory sales CTA or “who I help / transformation” language unless it fits the user's actual goals.
