# LinkedIn repurposing

Start by identifying the original thesis, evidence, intended reader, and what made the original valuable.

Adapt to the requested format (short post, longer essay, carousel outline, comment, newsletter, or reflection) by changing the explanatory route—not merely swapping synonyms.

- Preserve nuance and factual boundaries.
- Do not invent new examples, results, or audience reactions.
- Avoid publishing near-duplicate versions without a clear new purpose.
- Keep Kartik's established voice and professional positioning.
- For healthcare material, preserve safety boundaries and do not convert general education into individualized advice.

Output the adapted version and a short note describing what is genuinely new in the adaptation.
