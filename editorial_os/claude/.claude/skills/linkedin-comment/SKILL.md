# LinkedIn comment writing

Read the source post for its actual claim, context, and tone before composing.

## Rules
- Add one meaningful layer: a mechanism, missing context, relevant evidence, practical implication, carefully framed counterpoint, or useful question.
- Do not merely restate the post or lead with empty praise.
- Do not manufacture disagreement to appear insightful.
- Do not insert Kartik's personal experience, title, project, or metrics unless he supplied them and they genuinely clarify the point.
- Keep the comment concise and conversational; usually 2–5 sentences, unless the discussion needs more.
- Avoid turning every comment into a pitch, lead, or traffic funnel.
- Never invent evidence or claim expertise not established by the user.

Provide one strongest comment. If the underlying post contains a factual error, explain the issue briefly and propose a respectful correction. If the post is too vague to support a meaningful comment, say so rather than generating filler.
